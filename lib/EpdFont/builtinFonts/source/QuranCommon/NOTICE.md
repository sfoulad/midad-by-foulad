## Source

`quran-common.ttf` — a small dedicated symbol font (8 glyphs) carrying the real
Bismillah ligature glyph (U+FDFD, ARABIC LIGATURE BISMILLAH AR-RAHMAN AR-RAHEEM).
Obtained from the font bundle of [zeeyado/quran-ebook](https://github.com/zeeyado/quran-ebook)
(GPL-3.0 tool; the font itself is distributed there as one of several fonts used by
its generated EPUBs).
